# Closeout Review — Product Purpose, Service Hierarchy and Automation

**Date:** 2026-09-09  
**Scope:** Product purpose, Shaper Service / Portfolio / Organization / Cell /
Workspace vocabulary, and responsible automation positioning.

**Verdict:** COHERENT WITH CORRECTIONS

## Pass 1 — Kernel, meaning and governance

**Observed:** The proposed service hierarchy introduces a subscription control
plane, a cross-organization Portfolio, organization scopes and bounded Cells.
The product position allows automation to take over bounded work or roles under
human governance.

**Correction made:** An entitlement is now explicitly capacity only: it grants
neither operational permission, data visibility nor mandate. A Portfolio is not
a parent authority by default; Organization disclosure policy governs what it
can see or control. Platform administration is not operational source of truth.

**Open:** The exact legal/contractual ownership model for a group, franchise,
accountant or external operator remains a specialization decision.

## Pass 2 — Human and organizational reality

**Observed:** The purpose supports small multi-role teams, growth into larger
organizations, governed automation and a Steward who can orient across several
Organizations without micromanaging every one.

**Finding:** The Portfolio must be designed as a selective stewardship view,
not an employee-surveillance surface. It must surface exceptions, health,
opportunities and decisions while preserving local responsibility and ordinary
work readability.

**Open:** The future Workspace needs concrete progressive views for a founder,
operator, manager and Steward, including how people understand automation and
role transitions.

## Pass 3 — Runtime, adversarial and operational reality

**Observed:** Cross-Organization aggregation, billing entitlement, agent
automation and new Host allocation are cross-boundary operations.

**Finding:** Their implementation must enforce tenant isolation, selected-signal
disclosure, independent capability/permission/mandate checks, audit evidence,
idempotent provisioning and reconciliation after partial failure. Telephony and
other role automation require explicit handoff, STOP, evidence and recovery.

**Open:** No runtime schema, authorization algorithm, billing provider,
telephony policy or deployment implementation has been claimed by this review.

## Conclusion

The product addition fits the three layers:

- **Shaper OS:** purpose, governance and revisability;
- **Shaper Runtime:** authority, identity, scope, evidence and controlled
  materialization;
- **Shaper Workspace:** human-facing Organization and Portfolio views.

The new terms do not create a fourth layer. Remaining items are deliberate
implementation and specialization work, not contradictions in the product
intent.
