## Change in organization, not loss of substance

The earlier Enterprise OS “master layer” is no longer kept as one architectural bucket. Its content is redistributed:

- business state, identity, authority, events, workflows, RAG and automation → Runtime;
- human applications, surfaces and interactions → Workspace;
- adaptive governance and meta-regulation → Shaper OS;
- domain-specific functionality → packages/app schemas over Runtime/Workspace.

`40-TRANSVERSAL/09_ENTERPRISE_PRIMITIVES_MAPPING.md` records this mapping explicitly.
