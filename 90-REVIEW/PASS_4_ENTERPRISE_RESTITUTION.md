# Pass 4 — Enterprise Restitution, Helm and Testability

## Question
Did the three-layer refoundation preserve the lived enterprise proposition: examples, owner guidance, Helm, demonstration, validation and implementation criteria?

## Finding
The architecture and Enterprise primitive mapping were strong, but the primary reading path made the enterprise experience less visible. Helm appeared mainly as a deprecated historical term, while demo/test material survived mostly through lineage.

## Correction
This edition restores Enterprise OS as a reference manifestation—not a fourth layer—and restores the master, human path, agent guide, office-furniture company, functional graph, thirteen Helm scenarios, checklists, website explanation, future-owner/tester journey, canonical Helm model, acceptance protocol, Governor/Maker mapping and immutable source archive.

## Perspective verdicts

- Architecture: no contradiction; concerns resolve into OS, Runtime, Workspace and Packages.
- Human/business: the path is familiar work → ask → delegate → pilot → mandate → shape.
- Implementation: every promise connects to objects, authority, events, evidence, failure and recovery.
- Adversarial: Helm cannot become a hidden superuser; convenience does not bypass governance.

## Decision
Do not insert the old Enterprise kernel as a new monolith and do not restart the completed passes from zero. Preserve the source, retain the three-layer passes, restore the missing experience/test dimension, then review future deltas incrementally.
