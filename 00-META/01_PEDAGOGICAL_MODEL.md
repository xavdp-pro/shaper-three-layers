# Pedagogical Model

## Why pedagogy is architectural

A correct architecture can still fail if humans or agents cannot reconstruct why its rules exist. Shaper documentation therefore teaches the path that produces the rule, not only the rule itself.

The objective is not obedience to a doctrine. It is the ability to perceive, reason, act and revise without losing integrity.

## The three comprehension checks

### 1. Words
Before relying on a term, define it in the current context. Words such as *authority*, *trust*, *agent*, *workspace*, *object*, *root*, *secure*, *offline* and *automatic* are otherwise easy sources of false agreement.

### 2. Gradient
Do not jump from “user opens a document” directly to “fractal authority and causal event replay” without intermediate conceptual steps. Each level should be useful before the next level is introduced.

### 3. Reality
Reconnect every abstraction to at least one of:

- a concrete example;
- an observable behavior;
- a failure scenario;
- a test;
- a measurable consequence.

A concept that cannot yet be explained simply or connected to reality remains provisional.

## Five questions for every important concept

1. **What is it?**
2. **Why does it exist?**
3. **What does it look like in practice?**
4. **What can go wrong?**
5. **How do we verify it?**

These five questions are mandatory authoring checks for master and guide documents.

## Human Foundations path

The Human Foundations documents use a progressive route:

```text
REALITY
  ↓
MOVEMENT
  ↓
COMMUNICATION
  ↓
DISCERNMENT
  ↓
ETHICS
  ↓
TENSIONS / SENSORS
  ↓
DISTANCE / COUNTER-VIEW
  ↓
INTENTION / ACTION
  ↓
FEEDBACK / LEARNING
```

The reader first experiences why a concept is needed, then receives its name.

## Human Steward path

The Steward-level documents add:

```text
purpose
→ system boundaries
→ authority
→ trust roots
→ object/event model
→ policies
→ observability
→ failure containment
→ repair/rebuild
→ rollback
→ meta-regulation
```

A Steward should understand not merely *what screen to use*, but *which guarantees the system is trying to preserve*.

## Agent pedagogy

### A1 — Bounded Executor
Teach only what is necessary for reliable bounded action:

- objective;
- inputs;
- scope;
- output;
- success criterion;
- hard rules;
- STOP;
- escalation;
- trace.

A1 should not need to reconstruct the whole cathedral.

### A2 — Operational Reasoner
Add:

- state / tendency / intention;
- observation / interpretation / conclusion;
- provenance;
- hypotheses;
- discriminating tests;
- tensions and pluspoints;
- reversibility;
- counter-view;
- bounded adaptation.

### A3 — Systemic Reasoner
Add:

- goals and policies;
- multiple scales;
- hidden postulates;
- authority architecture;
- structural security;
- interactions among universes;
- feedback on feedback mechanisms;
- future branches;
- system evolution;
- creation and evaluation of sensors.

A3 can propose changes to the rules that produce decisions, but does not automatically receive permission to apply them.

## Response modes retained from earlier human documentation

The former H0/H1/H2/H3 response-depth model is retained, but renamed here to avoid confusing it with audience levels:

- **R0 — Immediate:** low-impact everyday response; notice, choose, continue.
- **R1 — Reflective:** meaningful/repeated tension; separate facts and interpretation, obtain a counter-view, act and review.
- **R2 — Deep:** persistent or major pattern; several independent views, small tests, longitudinal observation, model revision.
- **R3 — Safety/Crisis:** stabilize and protect first; deeper interpretation comes later.

These are **situation modes**, not user ranks.

## Learning success criterion

A guide succeeds when the reader or agent gains practical capability.

Ask:

> What can I now perceive, choose, test, create, stop, repair, or explain that I could not do before?

If the only result is stronger belief in the explanation, the teaching is incomplete.
