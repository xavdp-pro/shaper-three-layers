# Product Purpose and Service Model

> **Status:** foundational product intent. This document defines the direction
> for product language and service design; it does not claim implemented
> functionality or set final commercial names.

## Fundamental purpose

Shaper exists to help people and organizations **flourish and prosper through
constructive, positive and ethical value creation**.

It does not define prosperity as extraction, headcount replacement or blind
automation. It strengthens people's capacity to understand their work, act with
appropriate autonomy, learn across roles, cooperate and build durable economic
value.

Automation is not forbidden. An agent may remove drudgery, extend capability,
take over a bounded task or, where the organization deliberately chooses it,
operate a role whose work can be made safe, lawful and verifiable. The standard
is not preservation of every task: it is accountable transition, clear
authority, visible consequences and a net increase in the organization's and
its people's capacity to create value.

An agent is an assistant, collaborator or bounded operator under human
governance. It does not erase the human dignity, accountability, development or
livelihood that an organization must protect.

## What the service provides

Shaper provides a living, extendable organizational environment. A customer
starts from a coherent operational foundation — identity, authority, objects,
communications, work, evidence and selected business applications — then shapes
the additional objects, applications, cells or Universes that their reality
requires.

The foundation is not a fixed monolithic ERP. It is a governed system of
reusable components and durable relationships, able to grow with the organization
without forcing it to discard its history or conform to a rigid template.

## Service membership and roles

| Concern | Working name | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commercial service | **Shaper Service** | Subscription, entitlement, billing and requests. |
| Logical membership | **Space / Organization** | Services and relationships belonging to an enterprise, association, project or other jurisdiction. |
| Useful capability | **Specialized cell/service** | A base composed with domain units, such as telephony, CRM or a shop. |
| Interaction | **Shaper Workspace / Helm** | Permitted work surfaces and conversational piloting. |

A space is a logical collection of services; its members may run on different
hosts. Workspace presents those members. It is not the next box in a physical
containment chain. See [composition](08_COMPOSITION_AND_SOURCE_MAP.md),
[class/instance](09_CLASS_SPECIALIZATION_INSTANCE.md) and the
[independent glossary](https://github.com/xavdp-pro/shaper-glossary).

A jurisdiction grants a declared scope of responsibility and authority. It may
span several organizations or spaces, subject to their explicit disclosure and
control policies. Master/jurisdiction root roles remain governed by their scoped
mandate and [authority mapping](../40-TRANSVERSAL/10_HELM_GOVERNOR_MAKER_MAPPING.md).

## Growth model

A subscription grants a declared capacity: one or more Organizations, their
services, selected components, usage budgets and authorized execution environments.
An entitlement grants eligibility and capacity; it never grants operational
permission, data visibility or a mandate to act. Those remain Runtime authority
decisions inside the relevant Organization.

Governor coordinates approved work; Maker materializes approved infrastructure
changes. When capacity requires another host, the platform adds a governed Host
rather than changing the organizational model. Platform administration likewise
does not silently become Organization authority or operational source of truth.

This keeps growth incremental:

a selected class → authorized service instance → logical membership → permitted
Workspace projection. Commercial approval/entitlement, operational mandate,
governor desired state and maker outcome remain linked but distinct records.

The architecture scales by adding bounded, observable scopes — not by turning
one central instance into an opaque monolith.

## Product position

Shaper is not marketed as an “AI employee” factory. It is a **human-led
organizational operating environment**: business tools, collaboration, governed
automation and AI assistance connected to the actual context of the
organization.

The promise is not “replace your team.” It is:

> **Give your people a clearer shared environment in which they can create,
> coordinate, learn and prosper together.**
