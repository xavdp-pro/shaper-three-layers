# CRM route

Owning integration: [CRM](https://github.com/xavdp-pro/shaper-ecosystem/blob/main/chapters/60-CRM.md).
Existing domain basis: [relationships and communication](../50-ENTERPRISE-REFERENCE/12_COMMUNICATION_RELATIONSHIP_AND_SCHEDULING.md)
and [enterprise primitives](../40-TRANSVERSAL/09_ENTERPRISE_PRIMITIVES_MAPPING.md).

## Useful intention

People and agents understand relationships through connected parties, contacts,
communications, commitments and work. Customer and supplier are composable
relationship roles. Stable references and provenance preserve their history.

OS governs access and intended behavior. Runtime owns relationship identity and
workflow state. Workspace presents permitted context. The CRM specialized service
combines the generic base with selected domain units/schemas; an instance owns its
configuration and jurisdiction.

## Required dependencies and consumers

Resolve the party-identity owner, authorization, state and event contracts for the
task. Follow selected communication/task interfaces for related work.
[Telephony](TELEPHONY.md), [softphone](SOFTPHONE.md) and [shops](SHOPS.md) consume
permitted relationship references through those interfaces.

[MariaDB ownership](../40-TRANSVERSAL/17_APPLICATION_STATE_OWNERSHIP.md) preserves
service boundaries. Common mental sources connect to private user products,
configuration/data and separate private companion labs.
